## Overview

**B. J. Habibie — The Power of Ideas** is a tribute website dedicated to Bacharuddin Jusuf Habibie, Indonesia's third president, distinguished engineer, and prominent figure in the development of Indonesian science and technology.

Developed as part of the **Oasis Infobyte Internship Program — Web Development & Designing, Level 2**, this project presents his biography, educational background, professional journey, and contributions through a structured and visually engaging web experience.

The website combines editorial-style typography, historical storytelling, and a responsive layout to present the life and legacy of B. J. Habibie.

## About B. J. Habibie

Bacharuddin Jusuf Habibie was born in Parepare, South Sulawesi, on June 25, 1936. He was an Indonesian engineer and statesman who served as the third President of the Republic of Indonesia from 1998 to 1999.

Before entering national leadership, Habibie established an international career in aerospace engineering and contributed to the development of Indonesia's strategic technology and aircraft industries.

His educational background in Germany, engineering achievements, and leadership in science and technology made him one of Indonesia's influential figures in technological development.

He passed away on September 11, 2019, leaving a lasting legacy in Indonesian history.

## Features

### 2. Biography Section

- Introduction to Habibie's personal background.
- Overview of his education and engineering journey.
- Information about his professional career.
- Presentation of his contributions to Indonesia.

### 3. Historical Timeline

A chronological presentation of important milestones in Habibie's life and career, including:

- **1936:** Born in Parepare, South Sulawesi.
- **1956:** Began higher education at RWTH Aachen University in Germany.
- **1960:** Received his engineering diploma.
- **1965:** Earned his doctorate in aerospace engineering.
- **1974:** Returned to Indonesia.
- **1978:** Appointed Minister of Research and Technology.
- **1995:** N-250 Gatotkaca aircraft made its first flight.
- **1998:** Became the third President of Indonesia.
- **1999:** End of his presidential term.
- **2019:** Passed away on September 11.

### 4. Inspirational Quote

A dedicated section highlighting an inspirational message associated with Habibie's legacy, emphasizing the importance of knowledge, dedication, and contribution to the nation.

### 5. Legacy Section

- Recognition of his contributions to science and technology.
- Overview of his influence on Indonesia's aerospace industry.
- Reflection on his role in Indonesian national history.
- Closing section celebrating his legacy.

### 6. Navigation & User Experience

- Structured navigation between website sections.
- Smooth and intuitive browsing experience.
- Consistent typography and visual presentation.
- Responsive layout for different screen sizes.

## Installation & Setup

This project is a static website that can run directly in a modern web browser.

### 1. Clone the Repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/OIBSIP.git
```

### 2. Navigate to the Project Directory

```bash
cd OIBSIP/WebDev-L2-TributePage
```

### 3. Run the Application

Open `index.html` directly in your browser.

Alternatively, use the Visual Studio Code Live Server extension to run the website through a local development server.
